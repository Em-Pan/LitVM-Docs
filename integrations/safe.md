# Safe (Multisig)

> dApps and teams building on LitVM can use Safe to secure assets, manage treasuries, and govern protocols with institutional-grade multisig infrastructure.

Safe is the most trusted smart account platform in Web3, enabling multi-signature control of onchain assets. Rather than relying on a single private key, Safe requires a configurable threshold of signers to approve any transaction — eliminating single points of failure and ensuring collective control over funds and critical operations.

Safe is deployed and maintained on LitVM by [Protofire](https://protofire.io/), an official Safe partner and Safe Guardian with deployments across 130+ networks.

### Key Features

* Multi-signature security requiring M-of-N approvals before any transaction executes.
* Modular architecture supporting custom modules, guards, and spending limits.
* Full EVM compatibility — interact with any dApp or contract on LitVM directly from your Safe.
* Batch transactions to reduce gas costs by bundling multiple operations into a single signing step.
* Role-based access control for teams managing shared treasuries or protocol ownership.

## Getting Started

1. Visit [app.safe.global](https://app.safe.global).
2. Connect your wallet and select the **LitVM Testnet** network.
3. Click **Create new Safe** and configure your signers and threshold.
4. Fund your Safe with `zkLTC` for gas.
5. Start transacting — all transactions will require the configured number of approvals before execution.

## Use Cases

* **Protocol teams** — manage contract ownership and upgrades without single-key risk.
* **DAOs** — govern treasury funds with transparent, onchain multi-party approval.
* **DeFi protocols** — secure admin keys and parameter updates behind a multisig.
* **Builders** — deploy and manage contracts with team-controlled access.
